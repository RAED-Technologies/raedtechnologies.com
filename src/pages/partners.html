Questions

Before you send the first brief.

Will my client know you built it?

Only if you tell them. We work under your name, in your tools, and never contact your clients on our own.

Can we start with something small?

Yes. Many partnerships start with one feature or a short fix, so both sides can see how the other works before a larger project.

How do you handle time zones?

We are in Dhaka (GMT+6). We agree on a daily overlap window and a fixed time for updates, so nothing waits a full day for an answer.

Who owns the code and the designs?

You do, together with your client, once the work is delivered and paid for. We keep nothing back and reuse nothing confidential.

What do you need to give an estimate?

A short brief, any designs or references, and the deadline. If the scope is still open, we can help you shape it before you quote your client.
